About

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Fisk University. I received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Auburn University in 2024, where I was advised by Dr. Wei-Shinn (Jeff) Ku and Dr. Anh Nguyen. My research spans artificial intelligence, neural network robustness and interpretability, human motion analysis, generative models, and interdisciplinary applications in healthcare and bioinformatics. My recent work includes fine-grained sports action recognition, AI-based human motion analysis, large language models for functional genomics, and computational methods for predicting disease progression. My research has been published in venues including CVPR, ACCV, IEEE BigData, IEEE Access, and Briefings in Bioinformatics. My work at ACCV received the Huawei Best Application Paper Honorable Mention.
